Patient journey

Your treatment journey in Istanbul, step by step

Travelling for treatment is a big decision. We explain everything in advance and stay with you through one coordinator who speaks your language.

  • One coordinator from start to finish
  • A written plan and estimate before you travel
  • Follow-up after you return
Send your medical reportChat on WhatsApp
6 stages

From your first message to follow-up

Stage 1

Inquiry

Message us on WhatsApp, the form or email. A coordinator replies in your language and asks about your case and available documents.

  • +90 536 842 55 97
  • info@birunibridge.com
Stage 2

Report review

Send your reports on WhatsApp or by email. Physicians in the relevant specialty read your file and ask for any missing tests.

  • Usually within 24–48 hours of a complete file
  • Complex cases are discussed across specialties and may take longer
Stage 3

Plan & pricing

A written proposed plan and the expected length of stay.

  • The estimate states what is and is not included (e.g. complications or intensive care)
  • Pricing is case by case after reviewing your medical report.
Stage 4

Visa, travel, transfer & hotel

We send a medical invitation letter for your visa and arrange pick-up and accommodation for you and your companion.

  • Pick-up from Istanbul Airport or Sabiha Gökçen
  • Travel health insurance is a general visa requirement; we explain what applies to your country
Stage 5

Treatment

On arrival you are assessed and some tests may be repeated. The decision is explained in your language before anything starts.

  • Your coordinator and interpreter stay with you
  • Multidisciplinary decisions for complex cases
Stage 6

Follow-up

A detailed discharge summary for your doctor, a clear follow-up plan, and continued contact after you return home.

  • Remote follow-up
  • Contact with your local doctor

FAQ

Questions about the journey

Can someone come with me?

Yes. We arrange your companion’s stay, under the hospital’s companion policy.

Is there an interpreter?

Yes, an interpreter accompanies you during appointments and procedures. 24/7 availability

What if I need to stay longer?

Your coordinator tells you straight away and helps you extend accommodation and your visa if needed.

How do I pay?

By self-pay with a written estimate, or through your insurer: the hospital works with international insurance and assistance companies, with direct billing where an agreement exists. International insurance
