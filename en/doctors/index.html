FAQ

Questions about our physicians

Can I choose my doctor?

We route your file to the relevant specialty and the hospital assigns the treating physician according to the case. You can ask your coordinator any question about the team.

Which languages do the physicians speak?

Each physician’s languages are listed in the profile, and an interpreter accompanies you when needed.

Are the physicians faculty members?

Many are faculty members at Biruni University Faculty of Medicine; the academic title is shown on each card.
