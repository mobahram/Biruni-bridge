Medical disclaimer

Please read this before relying on any medical information on this website.

General information only

Content on this website is for general education. It is not a diagnosis, a treatment recommendation or a substitute for a consultation with a qualified physician.

No doctor–patient relationship

Contacting us through the website, WhatsApp or email does not create a doctor–patient relationship. Any opinion on your case is given only after physicians have reviewed your medical documents, and a final decision is made only after assessment at the hospital.

Results vary

Results of any treatment differ from patient to patient. We do not promise or guarantee any outcome. Descriptions of treatments, including bone marrow transplant and CAR-T cell therapy, explain how they generally work; eligibility is decided individually by the treating team.

Pricing

Pricing is case by case after reviewing your medical report. No price on social media or third-party sites should be treated as an offer from Biruni Bridge.

Emergencies

This website is not an emergency service. If your situation is urgent, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department.

Who decides

Only your treating physician can decide which treatment is appropriate for you. We do not list individual physicians. The partner hospital assigns your treating physician after it reviews your reports, and we share their details with you directly during coordination.
