Cookie policy

Cookie policy

We keep this website as light as possible: nothing that tracks you is loaded unless you agree.

Strictly necessary

We store your consent choice in your browser (localStorage key bb_consent) for up to 12 months so we do not ask again on every page. This is needed for the site to respect your choice and cannot be switched off.

Analytics

This website does not currently use analytics or advertising cookies. If analytics are added later, they will load only after you allow “Analytics” in the cookie settings, and this policy will be updated first.

Maps & external media

The map on the contact page is provided by Google Maps. It loads only after you allow “Maps & external media”; Google may then set its own cookies and process your IP address under its own privacy policy.

Fonts, hosting and forms

Fonts are served from our own server, not from third-party font services. The site is delivered by Netlify, which processes technical data (such as IP address) to serve and secure pages. The contact form is processed by Netlify Forms (see the Privacy & KVKK notice).

Changing your choice

Use “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page to change or withdraw your consent at any time.
