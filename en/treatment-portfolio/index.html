Frequently asked questions

What treatments and services does Biruni Hospital offer international patients?

Biruni Hospital's portfolio covers oncology and hematology (including CAR-T cell therapy and bone marrow transplantation), interventional oncology and nuclear medicine, liver and kidney transplantation, neurosurgery, cardiovascular surgery, urology, orthopedics and spine, ENT, gastroenterology, pediatrics, rehabilitation, intensive care, palliative care and reconstructive surgery.

Does Biruni Hospital offer CAR-T cell therapy?

Yes. CAR-T Cell Therapy is listed first in Biruni Hospital's Oncology & Hematology portfolio, alongside adult and pediatric bone marrow transplantation, medical, radiation and surgical oncology, HIPEC and cancer immunotherapy. Eligibility is decided by the treating medical team after reviewing the patient's file.

Can I get a second medical opinion from Biruni Hospital before travelling?

Second Medical Opinion Services are part of the hospital's additional services, supported by multidisciplinary tumor boards and international patient coordination. Biruni Bridge can help you submit your medical file for review.

Does Biruni Hospital treat children?

Yes. The portfolio includes pediatric surgery, neurosurgery, oncology, bone marrow transplantation, neurology and genetics, as well as pediatric rehabilitation and pediatric and neonatal intensive care units.

Which nuclear-medicine and interventional cancer treatments are available?

The list includes TARE (Y-90), TACE, cryoablation, radiofrequency ablation, Lutetium-177 PSMA therapy, Actinium-225 therapy, radioiodine (I-131) therapy and advanced PET/CT imaging.

How do I contact Biruni Bridge about a treatment?

Use the contact form on this site, or send your diagnosis summary and recent reports to the international patient team through Biruni Bridge, and they will coordinate the next steps with the hospital.

This portfolio is an overview of available services and is not medical advice. Suitability for any treatment is decided by the treating physicians.
