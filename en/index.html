FAQ

Questions patients often ask

How do I start?

Send your reports on WhatsApp or to info@birunibridge.com, or leave your details in the form. A coordinator who speaks your language contacts you and asks for anything missing.

What documents should I send?

Your latest medical report, imaging reports (DICOM images preferred), biopsy report if any, recent blood tests and a dated list of previous treatments.

When will I receive the plan?

Usually within 24–48 hours of a complete file. Complex cases may need more time to be discussed across specialties.

Do you help with visa and accommodation?

Yes. We send a medical invitation letter and arrange airport pick-up, transfer and accommodation for you and your companion.
