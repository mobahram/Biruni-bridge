International insurance

International insurance and direct billing

We help you deal with your insurer before you travel: pre-authorization, a written estimate, and direct billing where an agreement exists.

  • We contact your insurer on your behalf
  • A written estimate before any commitment

Ways to pay

Three clear routes

Direct billing

If your insurer has an agreement with the hospital, the bill goes directly to them after a guarantee of payment.

Pre-authorization, then reimbursement

We prepare the medical report and written estimate for your insurer, and you claim reimbursement.

Self-pay

A written estimate stating what is and is not included before you travel. Pricing is case by case after reviewing your medical report.

Travel health insurance

Many countries require travel health insurance for a visa. It is different from cover for planned treatment; we explain what applies to your country.

What we need from you

To speed up approval

FAQ

Insurance & cost questions

Do you work with my insurer?

Send the company name and policy number and we check for an active agreement.

How much does treatment cost?

Pricing is case by case after reviewing your medical report.

What does the estimate include?

It states what is and is not included, such as complications, intensive care or extra days.

Is my data shared with my insurer?

Only with your explicit consent, and only as needed for approval and billing.
