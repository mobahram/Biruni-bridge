Questions patients ask

Can my case be assessed from MRI alone?

It is often enough for a first assessment; more tests may be requested.

Is surgery always needed for a herniated disc?

No. Many cases improve without surgery.

How long will I stay after brain surgery?

It depends on the surgery and recovery.
