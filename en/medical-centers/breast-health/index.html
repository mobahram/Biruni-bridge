Questions patients ask

Can my mammogram from home be read?

Yes, send it in DICOM format with the report.

Will the biopsy need repeating?

Not always; existing slides and blocks may be re-read.

Can reconstruction be done in the same operation?

In some cases. The team decides based on the case and later treatment.
