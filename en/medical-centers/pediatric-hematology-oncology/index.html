About this center

Pediatric hematologist-oncologists work with pediatric surgery, child neurology, intensive care and the transplant team. We make sure the child and family understand every step, in their language.

Questions patients ask

Can parents stay with the child?

Yes, at least one parent, under hospital policy.

Which documents are needed?

Medical reports, biopsy or bone marrow report, recent tests and previous treatments.

Do you perform pediatric bone marrow transplants?

Yes, in the pediatric bone marrow transplant unit.
