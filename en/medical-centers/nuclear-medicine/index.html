About this center

Nuclear medicine uses small amounts of radioactive tracers to image how organs work, and to treat some conditions. It is central to staging many cancers and following response to treatment.

Imaging

PET/CT

Cancer staging and response assessment.

Gamma camera & SPECT/CT

Bone, heart and thyroid studies.

Cardiac imaging

Myocardial perfusion

Brain imaging

In selected neurological cases

Radionuclide treatment

Radioactive iodine

For some thyroid conditions

Targeted radionuclide therapy

For selected tumors after tumor board review

Questions patients ask

Is the radiation in PET/CT safe?

The dose is planned for diagnosis, and the team explains simple precautions after the scan.

Can it be compared with a previous scan from home?

Yes, bring the images in DICOM format.
