About this center

Cellular therapy uses living cells to treat disease. Its oldest and most established form is bone marrow (stem cell) transplant; CAR-T cell therapy is available at Biruni University Hospital for eligible patients, after evaluation by the team. We do not name commercial products and we do not promise any result.

CAR-T: general information

T cells are collected from the patient’s blood, modified in a laboratory to recognize a target on cancer cells, and returned in one infusion. It can help in some advanced blood cancers, does not work for everyone and carries serious risks that need specialist monitoring.

  • At Biruni: CAR-T cell therapy is available for eligible patients. The hematology and transplant team decides eligibility, timing and the plan after reviewing your file; not every patient is a candidate.
  • Pricing is case by case after reviewing your medical report.
  • Other cellular therapies may be available in selected cases within approved protocols after tumor board evaluation, alongside standard treatment, not instead of it TBC
  • No promise of cure and no guaranteed result: outcomes vary from patient to patient.

Questions patients ask

Is CAR-T cell therapy available at Biruni?

Yes, for eligible patients. The team decides eligibility after reviewing your file, and no result can be guaranteed.

Does CAR-T mean a complete cure?

A cure cannot be promised. Some patients achieve long remission; others do not respond or relapse.

Who is a suitable candidate?

Usually patients whose disease returned or did not respond to standard treatment, after a full assessment by Biruni’s team.

Which documents are needed?

Biopsy report with immunostaining, PET-CT reports, dated previous treatments and recent blood tests. Your coordinator tells you if anything else is needed.

How long must I stay near the hospital?

Usually weeks after infusion, with an adult companion. The team sets the exact period for your case before you travel.
