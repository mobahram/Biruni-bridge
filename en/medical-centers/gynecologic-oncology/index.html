About this center

Gynecologic oncologists work with medical oncology, radiotherapy, radiology and pathology. For younger women, fertility preservation is discussed early where possible, together with the IVF center.

Cancers we treat

Ovarian cancerEndometrial cancerCervical cancerVulvar cancerBorderline tumorsPre-cancerous lesions

Treatment

Surgery

Laparoscopic where suitable, or open surgery for advanced cases.

Drug treatment

Chemotherapy and targeted therapy.

Radiotherapy

External, sometimes internal

Fertility preservation

Egg freezing where possible

Questions patients ask

Can fertility be preserved?

Sometimes, depending on cancer type and stage. The team discusses it before treatment.

Will the biopsy be re-read?

Often yes when needed, so we ask for block and slide numbers.

How long will I stay?

It depends on the plan.
