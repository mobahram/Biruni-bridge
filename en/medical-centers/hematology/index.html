About this center

The hematology department diagnoses and treats disorders of blood cells, bone marrow and clotting in adults. Cases that may need a transplant are coordinated with the transplant center from the start.

Questions patients ask

Can my case be assessed from tests alone?

Sometimes. In many cases a bone marrow test or more tests at the hospital are needed.

Do you treat children?

Yes, through Pediatric Hematology-Oncology.

Will I need a transplant?

Not always. The physician decides based on the disease type and stage.
